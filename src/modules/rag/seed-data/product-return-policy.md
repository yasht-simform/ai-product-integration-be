# CloudPulse Subscription Return & Refund Policy

_Last updated: January 15, 2026_

CloudPulse is a cloud-based project management platform built for engineering and product teams.
This document describes our return, refund, and cancellation policy for all CloudPulse
subscription plans (Free, Pro, and Enterprise).

## Return Window

Customers who purchase a **Pro** or **Enterprise** subscription may request a full refund within
**30 days** of the original purchase date. This 30-day window applies to both monthly and annual
billing cycles and starts on the date the initial payment is charged, not the date the account was
created.

Refund requests made after the 30-day window are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by the billing
team and are not guaranteed.

## How to Request a Refund

1. Log in to your CloudPulse account and open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Request Refund** next to your active subscription.
3. Select a reason from the dropdown (pricing, missing features, technical issues, other).
4. Submit the form. You will receive a confirmation email within 24 hours.
5. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

Refunds can also be requested by emailing `billing@cloudpulse.io` with your account email and
workspace ID.

## Annual Plan Refunds

Annual Pro and Enterprise plans purchased at a discount (2 months free) are refundable in full
within the same 30-day window. After 30 days, annual plans are refundable on a **prorated basis**
only for the unused portion of the subscription term, minus a 10% early-cancellation fee.

## Non-Refundable Items

The following are **not eligible** for a refund under any circumstances:

- Custom integration development fees (one-time setup charges billed separately from the
  subscription)
- Enterprise onboarding and migration services
- Add-on storage purchases beyond the plan's included quota
- Any subscription that has been active for more than 12 months

## Downgrades vs. Refunds

Downgrading from Enterprise to Pro, or from Pro to Free, does not trigger an automatic refund.
Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, and the account retains its
current plan's features until that date. If you want a refund in addition to a downgrade, you must
submit a separate refund request within the 30-day window described above.

## Free Plan

The Free plan has no billing associated with it, so there is nothing to refund. Free workspaces
can be upgraded or deleted at any time from **Settings → Workspace → Delete Workspace**.

## Cancellation

Canceling a subscription stops future billing but does not automatically issue a refund for the
current billing period. Your workspace remains on its current plan until the end of the paid
period, after which it reverts to the Free plan. Data is retained for 90 days after cancellation
before being permanently deleted.

## Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your bank or card issuer instead of contacting CloudPulse
support first, your workspace will be suspended immediately pending resolution, and any custom
integrations will be disabled. We strongly encourage customers to contact
`billing@cloudpulse.io` before filing a chargeback — most billing issues are resolved within one
business day.

## Contact

For any billing question not covered here, contact the CloudPulse billing team at
`billing@cloudpulse.io` or open a support ticket from **Settings → Help → Contact Support**.
Average response time for billing tickets is under 4 hours during business days (9am–6pm ET).
