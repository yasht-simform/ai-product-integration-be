# CloudPulse Pricing Plans

CloudPulse offers three plans: **Free**, **Pro**, and **Enterprise**. All prices are billed in USD
per seat, per month. Annual billing gives a 2-month discount (pay for 10 months, get 12).

## Free Plan

**$0/month** — up to 5 team members per workspace.

- Up to 3 boards per workspace
- Up to 500 tasks total
- 1 GB file storage
- Community support (forum only, no SLA)
- API rate limit: 60 requests/minute
- 30-day activity history

## Pro Plan

**$12/user/month** (billed monthly) or **$10/user/month** (billed annually) — up to 50 team
members per workspace.

- Unlimited boards
- Unlimited tasks
- 50 GB file storage per workspace
- Email support with a 24-hour response SLA
- API rate limit: 300 requests/minute
- 1-year activity history
- Slack, GitHub, and Jira integrations
- Custom fields and automation rules (up to 20 active rules)

## Enterprise Plan

**Custom pricing** — starts at $25/user/month for 50+ seats, unlimited team members per
workspace.

- Everything in Pro, plus:
- Unlimited file storage
- Dedicated support with a 4-hour response SLA and a named account manager
- API rate limit: 2,000 requests/minute
- Unlimited activity history and audit log export
- SSO (SAML 2.0 and OIDC)
- Custom integration development support
- 99.9% uptime SLA with service credits for downtime
- Unlimited automation rules

## Add-ons

Available on Pro and Enterprise plans:

- **Extra storage**: $5/month per additional 10 GB (Pro only; Enterprise storage is unlimited)
- **Custom integration development**: one-time fee starting at $2,000, scoped per project
- **Priority onboarding**: $500 one-time, includes a dedicated 2-hour kickoff session

## Switching Plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are billed on a prorated basis for the remainder of the
current billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle — see the
Return & Refund Policy for details on refund eligibility when downgrading.

## Nonprofit and Education Discounts

Verified nonprofits and educational institutions receive 50% off Pro and Enterprise plans. Apply
at `cloudpulse.io/discounts` with proof of nonprofit or `.edu` status.
